## Upload defaults (YouTube Studio → Upload defaults)

- **Category:** Entertainment
- **Language:** English
- **License:** Standard
- **Visibility:** Private (pipeline flips public on schedule)
- **Comments:** On, hold potentially inappropriate for review
- **"Made for kids":** No. The stories are family-friendly, but MFK kills
  comments, end screens, notifications, and personalized ads — a growth
  killer. Family-friendly ≠ "made for kids" under COPPA as long as the
  channel doesn't primarily target under-13s.

## Naming conventions

- **Main titles:** `<Hero/Story>: <curiosity hook>` — e.g. *Hercules: The 12
  Impossible Chores That Made a God*
- **Shorts titles:** one wild fact + #Shorts — e.g. *He Cleaned 30 Years of
  Filth in One Day #Shorts*
- **Thumbnail text:** 2-4 words, never repeating the title
